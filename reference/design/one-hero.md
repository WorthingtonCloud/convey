# One hero per slide

Every slide has one thing the eye lands on first: the hero. A number at display size, a statement, a photo, a chart,
or one filled panel. The headline states the point; the hero proves or carries it; everything else is quieter.

- **One filled panel in the data color per slide:** the pick, the lead metric, the answer, the current part. The rest
  are raised panels. Two filled panels compete and neither wins.
- **One signal-colored group per slide:** a rule, a band, a set of discs, an emphasis word. The signal is an accent.
- **Quiet means smaller and in the body color,** not lighter or see-through.
- **Test it:** look at the slide in `sheet.png` (`convey look` draws every slide there at thumbnail size). If two things compete, shrink or quiet one.
  If nothing stands out, the slide has no point yet: fix the headline first.
