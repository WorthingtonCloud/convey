# Type scale

The Brand Profile sets the headline, statement, text and small sizes (tokens.css). Variants add display sizes for
moments. Sizes a variant may set, smallest to largest:

| Role | Size (px) | Line height |
|---|---|---|
| Labels, sources, eyebrows | 16 | 20 |
| Notes and evidence lines | 18 to 20 | 24 to 28 |
| Secondary heads | 22 to 26 | 28 to 32 |
| Column and panel heads | 28 to 32 | 32 to 36 |
| Display headlines and quotes | 56 to 88 | 60 to 92 |
| Hero numbers | 120 to 300 | 1.0 to 1.1 times the size |

- **Readable from the back of a room:** 18 px and up for anything the audience must read; 16 px only for labels and
  sources.
- **Each level at least about 1.5 times the one below,** so the order of reading is obvious at a glance.
- **Line heights on whole points** for any text that wraps (px x 0.75 a whole number: 20, 24, 28, 29.33, 32, 36, 40,
  44 ...). PowerPoint draws exact spacing in whole points, so others drift line by line.
- **Display type at 1.0 to 1.1 times its size.** Near 1.2 PowerPoint places big lines differently from the browser.
- **Count characters at display size:** a 288 px number fits about four characters; round the value the way people
  say it ("79%", "$83B") and give the exact figure in the evidence line.
- Heads and numbers in the heading face; sentences in the body face.
