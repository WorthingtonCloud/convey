# Photos

- **From the person's material only:** their site, product, people or problem, as a path in the project
  (`content.image`). Never stock imagery standing in for evidence, never a generated picture shown as real.
- **Type never sits on the picture.** A wash in the ground color fades into the photo under the text, or the text sits
  on the ground beside it. Without the wash, a busy patch eats the headline.
- **Crop with `focus`** (CSS `object-position`, "60% 40%") so the subject sits clear of the type.
- **One or two per deck:** opening, and the echo at the close. More dilute each other.
- **No photo:** use a cover / color-block, a statement or a section / hero-fade. Compose refuses a photo variant
  without one.
- Logos and icons are inline SVG, not photos, so they stay editable.
