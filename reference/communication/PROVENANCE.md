# Provenance

The five files in this folder (`evidence.md`, `story.md`, `audience.md`, `visual-grammar.md`, `quality.md`) consolidate a reference package written for this project: an earlier draft package, executive-deck-intelligence v0.1.

That package was itself a summary of public guidance. It drew on these kinds of sources:

- Answer-first writing in the style of the pyramid principle: a governing thought supported by grouped reasons.
- Public agent-skill documents on research synthesis, data storytelling, executive summaries, slide-deck structure, presentation workflow, and consulting-style analysis.
- Chart-choice vocabularies that map a data relationship to a family of chart forms, and published guidance on annotating charts.
- Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) contrast criteria, used as a design reference for slides.
- Microsoft's guidance on making PowerPoint presentations accessible, including reading order and its built-in checker.

The draft package summarized those ideas without copying their text.

Everything in this folder was then rewritten again, in new words and a new structure. No text was copied from the draft package or from any of its sources. Shared ideas, like leading with the answer or starting bar charts at zero, are common practice and are stated here in fresh phrasing.

The weights in the editorial score in `quality.md` are design choices. They are not research findings and have not been validated with viewers.
