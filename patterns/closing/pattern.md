# Pattern: closing

**Answers:** What should I do now?

## Content

```json
{"subtitle": "One sentence on what to do with it.", "kicker": "What to do next",
 "items": [{"head": "A next step", "text": "What it takes."}], "meta": "Where to go: a link, or who to call",
 "image": "sources/opening-photo.jpg"}
```

The deck's message or its ask as the headline, then what to do next: up to three steps, or one sentence, and where
to go. `image` repeats the opening photo for the echo variant.

## Variants

- **`skeleton`** (the template's title layout): the cover's skeleton, on the template's own closing or title layout.
- **`call-to-action`** (full-bleed): the ask at display size, up to three numbered next steps in a row, and where to go
  at the foot in the signal color.
- **`echo`** (full-bleed): the opening photo again under a wash in the ground color, the closing line and where to go
  on the wash. Needs `image`.

## What suits it

The ask, said once more, with something the audience can do the next day. An informational deck still ends on what
to do with what they now know.

## Density

- **Live:** the ask; say the steps.
- **Read-ahead:** the steps and where to go.

## Common failures

- "Questions?" or "Thank you" as the last slide: it throws away the last thing the room sees.
- A new claim on the closing slide.
